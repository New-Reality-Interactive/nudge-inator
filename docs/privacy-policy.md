# Nudge-inator Privacy Policy

Last updated: [PLACEHOLDER: date of publishing]

Nudge-inator is an iPhone app that reminds you of things until you mark them done. This page
says what happens to your information.

## The short version

- Nudge-inator collects no data about you.
- Nudge-inator makes no network connections.
- Your reminders stay on your iPhone.

## What Nudge-inator collects

Nothing. The app has no account and no server. It has no ads and no analytics. It does not track
you. It does not share anything with anyone, because it never sends anything off your iPhone.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your reminders, tags and history are saved on your iPhone. They do not sync to other devices.

They stay there until you delete them. You can delete one reminder at a time. You can also
delete everything at once (see "How to delete your data" below).

Your reminders are part of your own iPhone backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to
a computer, the backup can include them. Apple controls those backups, not Nudge-inator. You
manage them in your iPhone's settings.

## How to delete your data

1. Open Nudge-inator.
2. Go to Settings.
3. Tap Delete All Data, then tap Delete.

This deletes every reminder, tag and all history from your iPhone. It also cancels every nudge
and alarm. It cannot be undone.

If you want to remove the app, do this first. Deleting the app alone leaves its alarms
scheduled on your iPhone. Use Delete All Data first, then delete the app.

## Permissions

Nudge-inator asks to send notifications and to set alarms. It uses them only to remind you on
your iPhone. You can turn either off in your iPhone's Settings.

## Links

Settings › About has links to this page and to the support page. When you tap one, your
iPhone opens your web browser. The page you visit is a web page, not part of the app. You start
this yourself, and the app sends nothing when you do.

## TestFlight

Some people try Nudge-inator before its release through Apple's TestFlight. TestFlight is
Apple's service, not Nudge-inator's. If you use it:

- Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information, and shares them with the
  developer. You cannot turn this off.
- If you send feedback from TestFlight, the developer receives it. Feedback can include your
  comments, screenshots, and details about your device, such as its model, iOS version,
  carrier, time zone and connection type. Apple says it may be linked to your name and email
  address.
- A screenshot shows whatever is on your screen, including your reminder text. Crop it or
  choose a different screen before you send it.
- The developer does not share this information with anyone else.

## Children

Nudge-inator collects no data from anyone, including children.
