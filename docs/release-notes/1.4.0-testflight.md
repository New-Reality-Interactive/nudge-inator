# 1.4.0 TestFlight: What to Test

Paste the text below into the build's **What to Test** in App Store Connect when uploading 1.4.0
(shared-reminders spec, #127). TestFlight shows it to the testers before they install, and it's
plain text, so the block is written without Markdown. The highlights are the changes since 1.3.0; the
coming-soon list is drawn from GitHub's open milestones that link a spec (`docs/agents/release.md`).

```text
Pick the steps that match you.

IF YOU'RE UPDATING FROM 1.1.0, 1.2.0 OR 1.3.0:
Install this version from TestFlight over the old one. Your reminders, tags and settings carry over, and so do your pending nudges and alarms.

IF YOU USED A VERSION BEFORE 1.1.0:
1. Open Nudge-inator, go to Settings, tap Delete All Data, then Delete. This cancels every nudge and alarm. Deleting the app alone leaves its alarms behind.
2. Delete Nudge-inator from your iPhone.
3. Install this version from TestFlight.

IF YOU'VE NEVER USED NUDGE-INATOR:
When the app asks, allow notifications, Time Sensitive notifications and alarms. Reminders need them to reach you.

TO TRY SHARING:
Sharing needs two people, each with their own Apple Account and Nudge-inator installed from TestFlight. Install the build from TestFlight before you open an invite: an invite can't lead to an App Store page yet. Then turn on Sharing in Settings, tap Connections and Invite Someone, and use the email address or phone number of the other person's Apple Account. Once they accept, add them to a reminder with Send To.

WHAT'S NEW SINCE 1.3.0:
- Send a reminder to someone you trust. Invite them through iCloud, then send them reminders at any strength, Relentless included. Each reminder nudges them on their iPhone as their own do, labeled From and your name.
- You choose how each person's reminders arrive: right away, or only after you accept them.
- You can mark a received reminder Done, pause it or remove it. The sender owns its words, time and strength, and a change they make reaches your copy with a note.
- The Reminders tab has a new Sent view. It shows each reminder you sent, grouped by person, with its status (Delivered, Done, Paused, Missed or Removed) when that person shares status, and the last 7 days of a repeating one.
- Sharing is off until you turn it on in Settings. A short guide, How Sharing Works, opens the first time and stays in Settings.
- Disconnect from someone and remove their reminders, or keep them as your own.
- Share Activity in Settings lists what iCloud refused or lost.
- Your own reminders on the Reminders tab swipe to Edit as well as Done and Delete.

COMING SOON:
- 1.4.1: fixes for what you find in shared reminders.
- 1.5.0: pick the sound your alarms ring with, from six tones or the standard one.
- 1.6.0: set quiet hours, when no nudge is delivered.
- 1.7.0: set a nudge by talking to Siri.
- 1.8.0: get your nudges on an Apple Watch.
- 1.9.0: choose the app's accent color from Settings.
- 1.10.0: the app in Spanish, Arabic, Hindi and Gujarati.
Tell us what you'd change about any of them.

This build lasts 90 days after it is uploaded. After that, TestFlight stops it from opening.

What TestFlight shares: TestFlight sends crash logs and usage information to the developer. Apple collects them automatically, and you can't turn this off. If you send feedback, the developer also gets your comment, your screenshot and details about your device. Nudge-inator itself collects nothing and has no server of its own. If you turn on Sharing, the reminders you send and receive go through your iCloud.

To send feedback, take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback. A screenshot shows everything on the screen, including the text of your reminders. Crop it or pick a screen that has nothing private before you send it.
```
