# 1.5.0 TestFlight: What to Test

Paste the text below into the build's **What to Test** in App Store Connect when uploading 1.5.0
(alarm sounds spec, #129). TestFlight shows it to the testers before they install, and it's plain
text, so the block is written without Markdown. The highlights are the changes since 1.4.0; the
coming-soon list is drawn from GitHub's open milestones that link a spec (`docs/agents/release.md`).

```text
Pick the steps that match you.

IF YOU'RE UPDATING FROM 1.1.0, 1.2.0, 1.3.0 OR 1.4.0:
Install this version from TestFlight over the old one. Your reminders, tags, connections and settings carry over, and so do your pending nudges and alarms. Your reminders keep ringing with the standard alarm sound until you pick another.

IF YOU USED A VERSION BEFORE 1.1.0:
1. Open Nudge-inator, go to Settings, tap Delete All Data, then Delete. This cancels every nudge and alarm. Deleting the app alone leaves its alarms behind.
2. Delete Nudge-inator from your iPhone.
3. Install this version from TestFlight.

IF YOU'VE NEVER USED NUDGE-INATOR:
When the app asks, allow notifications, Time Sensitive notifications and alarms. Reminders need them to reach you.

TO TRY SHARING:
Sharing needs two people, each with their own Apple Account and the latest Nudge-inator installed from TestFlight. Install the build from TestFlight before you open an invite: an invite can't lead to an App Store page yet. Then turn on Sharing in Settings, tap Connections and Invite Someone, and use the email address or phone number of the other person's Apple Account. Once they accept, add them to a reminder with Send To.

WHAT'S NEW SINCE 1.4.0:
- Pick the sound your alarms ring with: six new tones (Chime, Bell, Pulse, Beacon, Rise and Siren) or iOS's own Default Alarm.
- Settings has a Default Alarm Sound. New reminders, and reminders someone sends you, start with it. Tap a tone to hear it.
- Each reminder that rings has a Sound row on its form, so one reminder can sound different from the rest, even while it's nudging. Its details page names its sound.
- Use for All Reminders, on the Default Alarm Sound page, gives every reminder you have the same sound at once.
- The sound plays only when a nudge rings as an alarm. Other nudges keep the standard notification sound.
- Settings has a new Tutorials section: How It Nudges, How Sharing Works and the new How Alarm Sounds Work, each a short guide you swipe through.
- An invite's help now says that both people need the latest Nudge-inator.

COMING SOON:
- 1.6.0: set quiet hours, when no nudge is delivered.
- 1.7.0: set a nudge by talking to Siri.
- 1.8.0: get your nudges on an Apple Watch.
- 1.9.0: choose the app's accent color from Settings.
- 1.10.0: the app in Spanish, Arabic, Hindi and Gujarati.
- 1.12.0: an optional one-time Pro unlock for sharing with more than one person, Apple Watch, Siri and accent color. Everything else stays free.
Tell us what you'd change about any of them.

This build lasts 90 days after it is uploaded. After that, TestFlight stops it from opening.

What TestFlight shares: TestFlight sends crash logs and usage information to the developer. Apple collects them automatically, and you can't turn this off. If you send feedback, the developer also gets your comment, your screenshot and details about your device. Nudge-inator itself collects nothing and has no server of its own. If you turn on Sharing, the reminders you send and receive go through your iCloud.

To send feedback, take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback. A screenshot shows everything on the screen, including the text of your reminders. Crop it or pick a screen that has nothing private before you send it.
```
