# 1.6.0 Beta App Review: Review Notes

Paste the text below into App Store Connect's Review Notes field for 1.6.0 (quiet hours and
shortcuts spec, #338). It starts from [1.5.0's](1.5.0-review-notes.md) and adds what 1.6.0 changes.
Plain text, at most 4,000 characters, with no demo account and no contact details
(`docs/agents/release.md`).

```text
Nudge-inator 1.6.0 adds quiet hours, when no nudge is delivered, and a few shortcuts. No sign-in or demo account is needed.

Quiet hours:
1. Open Settings and tap Quiet Hours. The first time, the How Quiet Hours Work tutorial opens over the page; swipe through it or tap Close.
2. Tap the plus button (Add Schedule), choose a start, an end and the days, and tap Add. While a schedule that is on covers the time, no nudge is delivered: no notification, no Time Sensitive notification and no alarm. Each schedule has a switch to turn it off without deleting it.
3. Turn on Quiet Now, at the top of the page, to hold every nudge until it is turned off. Now, My Day and Reminders then show a banner saying quiet hours are holding nudges, and until when.
4. A held nudge counts as one the person didn't answer. Nothing is delivered late. A reminder that reaches its give-up limit while its nudges are held closes as Missed, and its details say how many nudges quiet hours held.
To see it without waiting: add a reminder due two minutes from now, turn on Quiet Now, and note that nothing arrives when it comes due. Turn Quiet Now off and the next nudge on its schedule arrives.

Settings: the Notifications, Alarms and Badges rows open the app's page in iOS Settings.

My Day: the arrows either side of the date show the previous or next day, back to the day the oldest reminder was created and ahead 7 days. Today returns to today.

Tags: tapping a tag opens its own page with its counts, a Reminders row, Edit and Delete Tag. Swiping a tag row offers Delete, Edit and List (its reminders). Tags and the people on Sent are listed in the order of the device's language.

Alarm sounds (added in 1.5.0): Settings › Default Alarm Sound chooses the sound alarms ring with, from iOS's Default Alarm and six tones made for the app with no third-party audio. A reminder that rings as an alarm has its own Sound row.

Settings has a Tutorials section: How It Nudges, How Sharing Works, How Alarm Sounds Work and How Quiet Hours Work, each a short guide of a few pages.

Shared reminders (added in 1.4.0) are off until the person turns on Sharing in Settings.

Sharing needs two people, each with their own Apple Account and Nudge-inator installed from TestFlight.
To try it:
1. On the first iPhone, open Settings, turn on Sharing, tap Connections, then Invite Someone.
2. Enter the email address or phone number of the second person's Apple Account and send the invite.
3. On the second iPhone, install the build from TestFlight first, then open the invite and accept it.
4. On the first iPhone, add a reminder, choose Send To and pick the second person. The reminder arrives on the second iPhone and nudges there.

Sharing goes through the people's own iCloud accounts. The app has no account system and no server of its own.
Reminders that are not shared stay on the iPhone. No sign-in is needed to review the rest of the app.
```
