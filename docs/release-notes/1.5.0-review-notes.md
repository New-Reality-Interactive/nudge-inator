# 1.5.0 Beta App Review: Review Notes

Paste the text below into App Store Connect's Review Notes field for 1.5.0 (alarm sounds spec,
#129). It starts from [1.4.0's](1.4.0-review-notes.md) and adds what 1.5.0 changes. Plain text, at
most 4,000 characters, with no demo account and no contact details (`docs/agents/release.md`).

```text
Nudge-inator 1.5.0 lets the person choose the sound their alarms ring with. No sign-in or demo account is needed.

Alarm sounds:
1. Open Settings, tap Default Alarm Sound. The list has Default Alarm (iOS's own alarm sound) and six tones that ship in the app: Chime, Bell, Pulse, Beacon, Rise and Siren. Tapping a tone plays a preview. New reminders start with the sound chosen here.
2. Tap the plus button (New Reminder), set Strength to Relentless, and tap Sound to choose a tone for that one reminder. The Sound row shows only on reminders that ring as alarms: Relentless, or Firm with Break Through Focus on.
3. Set the reminder's Starts to two minutes from now and save it. When it comes due, it rings as an AlarmKit alarm with the chosen tone.
4. Use for All Reminders, on the Default Alarm Sound page, gives every existing reminder the default sound, after asking.
The six tones were made for the app from scratch, with no third-party audio. The sound plays only for alarms; notification nudges keep the standard notification sound.

Settings has a new Tutorials section: How It Nudges, How Sharing Works and How Alarm Sounds Work, each a short guide of a few pages.

Shared reminders (added in 1.4.0) are off until the person turns on Sharing in Settings.

Sharing needs two people, each with their own Apple Account and Nudge-inator installed from TestFlight.
To try it:
1. On the first iPhone, open Settings, turn on Sharing, tap Connections, then Invite Someone.
2. Enter the email address or phone number of the second person's Apple Account and send the invite.
3. On the second iPhone, install the build from TestFlight first, then open the invite and accept it.
4. On the first iPhone, add a reminder, choose Send To and pick the second person. The reminder arrives on the second iPhone and nudges there.

Sharing goes through the people's own iCloud accounts. The app has no account system and no server of its own.
Reminders that are not shared stay on the iPhone. No sign-in is needed to review the rest of the app.
```
