# 1.6.0 TestFlight: What to Test

Paste the text below into the build's **What to Test** in App Store Connect when uploading 1.6.0
(quiet hours and shortcuts spec, #338). TestFlight shows it to the testers before they install, and
it's plain text, so the block is written without Markdown. The highlights are the changes since
1.5.0; the coming-soon list is drawn from GitHub's open milestones that link a spec
(`docs/agents/release.md`).

```text
Pick the steps that match you.

IF YOU'RE UPDATING FROM 1.1.0, 1.2.0, 1.3.0, 1.4.0 OR 1.5.0:
Install this version from TestFlight over the old one. Your reminders, tags, connections and settings carry over, and so do your pending nudges and alarms. Quiet hours start off, so nothing is held until you set them up.

IF YOU'VE NEVER USED NUDGE-INATOR:
When the app asks, allow notifications, Time Sensitive notifications and alarms. Reminders need them to reach you.

TO TRY SHARING:
Sharing needs two people, each with their own Apple Account and the latest Nudge-inator installed from TestFlight. Install the build from TestFlight before you open an invite: an invite can't lead to an App Store page yet. Then turn on Sharing in Settings, tap Connections and Invite Someone, and use the email address or phone number of the other person's Apple Account. Once they accept, add them to a reminder with Send To.

WHAT'S NEW SINCE 1.5.0:
- Quiet hours: in Settings, under Quiet Hours, add schedules (a start, an end and the days) when no nudge reaches you, alarms included.
- Quiet Now, at the top of that page, holds every nudge until you turn it off.
- A nudge that falls in quiet hours counts as one you didn't answer. Nothing arrives late; the next nudge after quiet hours comes as planned.
- While quiet hours are holding nudges, Now, My Day and Reminders say so, and until when.
- A reminder that gave up during quiet hours closes as Missed, and its details say how many of its nudges were held.
- How Quiet Hours Work, a new tutorial, opens the first time you open Quiet Hours, and stays in Settings' Tutorials.
- The Notifications, Alarms and Badges rows in Settings now open the app's page in iOS Settings.
- My Day shows other days: use the back and forward arrows to go back to the day of your oldest reminder, or ahead 7 days. Today brings you back.
- Tapping a tag on the Tags tab opens its own page, with its counts, its reminders, Edit and Delete Tag. Swiping a tag row offers List, which opens its reminders.
- The Tags tab, and the people on Sent, now list names in the order your language uses, and "Tag 2" comes before "Tag 10".

COMING SOON:
- 1.7.0: set a nudge by talking to Siri.
- 1.8.0: get your nudges on an Apple Watch.
- 1.9.0: choose the app's accent color from Settings.
- 1.10.0: the app in Spanish, Arabic, Hindi and Gujarati.
- 1.12.0: an optional one-time Pro unlock for sharing with more than one person, Apple Watch, Siri and accent color. Everything else stays free.
Tell us what you'd change about any of them.

This build lasts 90 days after it is uploaded. After that, TestFlight stops it from opening.

What TestFlight shares: TestFlight sends crash logs and usage information to the developer. Apple collects them automatically, and you can't turn this off. If you send feedback, the developer also gets your comment, your screenshot and details about your device. Nudge-inator itself collects nothing and has no server of its own. If you turn on Sharing, the reminders you send and receive go through your iCloud.

To send feedback, take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback. A screenshot shows everything on the screen, including the text of your reminders. Crop it or pick a screen that has nothing private before you send it.
```
