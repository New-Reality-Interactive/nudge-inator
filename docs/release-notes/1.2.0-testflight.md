# 1.2.0 TestFlight: What to Test

Paste the text below into the build's **What to Test** in App Store Connect when uploading 1.2.0
(icon-badge spec, decision 16). TestFlight shows it to the testers before they install, and it's
plain text, so the block is written without Markdown.

```text
Pick the steps that match you.

IF YOU'RE UPDATING FROM 1.1.0:
Install this version from TestFlight over the old one. Your reminders and settings carry over, and the badge needs no prompt: it turns on the next time you open the app.

IF YOU USED A VERSION BEFORE 1.1.0:
1. Open Nudge-inator, go to Settings, tap Delete All Data, then Delete. This cancels every nudge and alarm. Deleting the app alone leaves its alarms behind.
2. Delete Nudge-inator from your iPhone.
3. Install this version from TestFlight.

IF YOU'VE NEVER USED NUDGE-INATOR:
When the app asks, allow notifications, Time Sensitive notifications and alarms. Reminders need them to reach you. The notification prompt now includes badges.

What's new: the Nudge-inator icon on your Home Screen shows a badge with how many reminders are nudging now, the same number as the Now tab. Stop on an alarm doesn't clear it. The badge drops when a reminder is marked Done, or Missed or Skipped. If badges are off for the app, Settings shows a Badges row saying so.

The badge is best effort while the app isn't running. A Relentless reminder that rings as an alarm may not raise it until you open the app. A reminder that just Missed can keep counting until the app opens or the next notification arrives.

This build lasts 90 days after it is uploaded. After that, TestFlight stops it from opening.

What TestFlight shares: TestFlight sends crash logs and usage information to the developer. Apple collects them automatically, and you can't turn this off. If you send feedback, the developer also gets your comment, your screenshot and details about your device. Nudge-inator itself collects nothing and makes no network connections.

To send feedback, take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback. A screenshot shows everything on the screen, including the text of your reminders. Crop it or pick a screen that has nothing private before you send it.
```
