# 1.3.0 TestFlight: What to Test

Paste the text below into the build's **What to Test** in App Store Connect when uploading 1.3.0
(reminders-tab spec, #104). TestFlight shows it to the testers before they install, and it's
plain text, so the block is written without Markdown. The highlights are the changes since 1.2.0; the
coming-soon list is drawn from GitHub's open milestones that link a spec (`docs/agents/release.md`).

```text
Pick the steps that match you.

IF YOU'RE UPDATING FROM 1.1.0 OR 1.2.0:
Install this version from TestFlight over the old one. Your reminders, tags and settings carry over. Each reminder you already have is given a created date, the moment you first open this version, in the order they were saved.

IF YOU USED A VERSION BEFORE 1.1.0:
1. Open Nudge-inator, go to Settings, tap Delete All Data, then Delete. This cancels every nudge and alarm. Deleting the app alone leaves its alarms behind.
2. Delete Nudge-inator from your iPhone.
3. Install this version from TestFlight.

IF YOU'VE NEVER USED NUDGE-INATOR:
When the app asks, allow notifications, Time Sensitive notifications and alarms. Reminders need them to reach you.

WHAT'S NEW SINCE 1.2.0:
- A new Reminders tab, between My Day and Tags, lists every reminder you have in any state: Nudging, Coming up, Paused, Done, Missed or Skipped. Search it by title or notes, filter it by tags, status, strength or a range of due dates, and sort it by when it was created or when it's due. Swipe a row to mark it Done or to delete it.
- The Tags tab gets the same tools: a count, search, a filter for tags that have no reminders, a sort, and a swipe to rename or delete a tag.
- In the reminder form, Repeat is now a menu. Choose Custom for more, which opens as a sheet you close with the check mark. A new End Repeat row ends a repeat after a number of times or on a date.
- Questions before deleting now appear as a small popover, and the one that asks before you discard changes shows its title.
- A ringing alarm now stops when you delete the reminder, mark it Done, pause it or delete all data. Deleting a reminder also removes its notifications that are already on your screen.

COMING SOON:
- 1.4.0: send a reminder to someone you trust over iCloud, and it nudges them on their iPhone as their own do.
- 1.5.0: pick the sound your alarms ring with, from six tones or the standard one.
- 1.6.0: choose the app's accent color from Settings.
- 1.7.0: the app in Spanish, Arabic, Hindi and Gujarati.
- 1.8.0: a pass over the app's accessibility, tested against Apple's Accessibility Nutrition Label criteria.
Tell us what you'd change about any of them.

This build lasts 90 days after it is uploaded. After that, TestFlight stops it from opening.

What TestFlight shares: TestFlight sends crash logs and usage information to the developer. Apple collects them automatically, and you can't turn this off. If you send feedback, the developer also gets your comment, your screenshot and details about your device. Nudge-inator itself collects nothing and makes no network connections.

To send feedback, take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback. A screenshot shows everything on the screen, including the text of your reminders. Crop it or pick a screen that has nothing private before you send it.
```
