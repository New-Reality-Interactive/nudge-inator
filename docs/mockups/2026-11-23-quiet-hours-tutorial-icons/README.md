# Nudge-inator iOS mockup: How Quiet Hours Work, Your Schedules with icons (#379)

A change mockup for quiet-hours-and-shortcuts (spec #338): a copy of the
[How Quiet Hours Work tutorial mockup](../2026-11-22-quiet-hours-tutorial/README.md) with one page
changed, and nothing else. It is deleted when the 1.6.0 version mockup takes the change in.

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser. It's a single file with no dependencies. The panel's
**How Quiet Hours Work (#468)** group has a new button, **page 3 (Your Schedules)**, that opens the
tutorial on the changed page.

## What changed since the tutorial mockup

- **Your Schedules (page 3) draws its three rows with icons, not numbers.** The phone check found the
  numbered circles out of place: the other tutorial pages list their rows with an icon in a tile, and
  these three are not a sequence (adding, changing and switching off are independent). The rows keep
  their titles and text: **Add a Schedule** (`plus`), **Change One** (`pencil`) and **Switch One Off**
  (`switch.2`), under the same lead and above the same note.  How Sharing Works' Connect page keeps its numbered steps, which are a sequence.
