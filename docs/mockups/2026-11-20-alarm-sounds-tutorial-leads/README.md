# Nudge-inator iOS mockup: How Alarm Sounds Work leads

A change mockup for `enhancement-alarm-sound` (spec #129), from #467's phone check (2026-11-20).
Copied from the [tutorial sheets mockup](../2026-11-20-tutorial-sheets/README.md), with only this
changed, so the two can be compared screen by screen. The 1.5.0 version mockup takes it in and
replaces it.

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser. The panel's **Tutorials (#465)** group opens How Alarm
Sounds Work.

## What changed since the tutorial sheets mockup

- **Change Your Reminders** gains a lead under its heading: "Change the sound of reminders you
  already have, all at once or one at a time."
- **When It Plays** gains a lead under its heading: "Your sound plays only when a nudge rings as an
  alarm." Its picture is the Alarm pill alone: the Notification pill is gone, since a notification
  never plays the sound and the pill beside it read as if it did.

Nothing else changed.
