# Nudge-inator iOS mockup: Sharing privacy copy

The change mockup for the Settings footer's new wording (#213, spec #127, version 1.4.0), copied from
the [account-change mockup](../2026-11-13-account-change/README.md), the latest of the feature's
change mockups. It's deleted when the 1.4.0 version mockup takes the change in.

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser, then Settings, under Your Data, and the Onboarding page and How It Nudges section "Stays on your iPhone".

## What changed since the account-change mockup

- The Settings footer under Your Data now reads: "Your own reminders stay on this iPhone. Reminders
  you send or receive via an approved share, go through your iCloud. There's no account and no
  server of ours involved."
- Onboarding and How It Nudges, "Stays on your iPhone", says the same, replacing "No account and no server. Your reminders stay on this iPhone and in your own backups."
